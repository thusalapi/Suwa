Health, managed.

Suwa is clinic management software for small medical centres and laboratories. Register patients, enter lab results into ready-made report templates, and print reports and bills — all in one place.

Lab reports

Staff type only the measured results. Suwa works out the calculated values, flags results outside the reference range, and prints a report in the clinic's own layout.

Billing and receipts

Itemised bills, discounts and part-payments, with A4 invoices and 80 mm receipt printing.

Patient visits

Each patient's reports and bills are grouped by visit date, so a day's work is easy to find.

Private by design

Each clinic's system is reachable only by its own staff, over a private network. Nightly encrypted backups protect the data.

Who it is for

Suwa is used by clinic and laboratory staff, and by the doctors and owners who run them. Patients do not use it directly. Accounts are created by the clinic; there is no public sign-up.
